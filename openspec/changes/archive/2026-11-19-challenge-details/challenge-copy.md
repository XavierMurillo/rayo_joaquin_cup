# Version III challenge instructions

Draft for organizer review. The titles are proposed for the student-facing page. Each challenge is paired below with the corresponding technical module from the preparation guidelines.

## Challenge 1 — Lead and Follow

**Module A — Real-Time Visual Tracking & Closed-Loop Control**

Two vehicles run together. The lead vehicle drives the assigned movement pattern. The following vehicle tracks the leader and tries to maintain the assigned distance, measured in centimeters, throughout the run. Configure the vehicle for both roles. The winner is the vehicle with the best combined performance as leader and follower.

**Awaiting release.** The movement pattern, target distance, starting positions, and how lead and follow performance are measured and combined.

## Challenge 2 — Recognize and React

**Module B — Visual State Machine & Timed Actuation**

The vehicle reads visual cues made up of colors and shapes. It identifies the cue and executes the action assigned to that color and shape. Be ready to configure which action the vehicle performs for each cue. If cues are presented as a sequence, the published procedure will explain when to start executing the actions.

**Awaiting release.** The colors and shapes, the action assigned to each cue, how cues will be presented, and how correct recognition and execution will be scored.

## Challenge 3 — Clear the Arena

**Module C — Reactive Arena Navigation & Perimeter Boundary Safety**

Two teams compete, each with one vehicle in its own arena. Find and clear every object that matches the announced target color or shape while keeping your vehicle inside its boundary. The first team to clear all its targets wins.

**Awaiting release.** The target, arena layout, starting positions, how an object counts as cleared, the time limit, and how boundary violations affect the result.

## Challenge 4 — Vehicle Soccer

**Module D — Real-Time Wireless Teleoperation**

Drive your vehicle by remote control and try to score by moving the ball into the opposing goal. Prepare your wireless controls for quick changes in direction and speed so you can approach the ball, line up a shot, and defend your goal.

**Awaiting release.** The match format, vehicle count, ball and field setup, scoring and restart rules, and permitted contact.

## Release decisions for all challenges

Confirm what teams may change between runs, how much setup time they have, and whether intervention during a run is allowed. For any reference diagram, identify which details represent the actual setup and which are illustrative.
